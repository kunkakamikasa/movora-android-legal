Movora Privacy Policy

Android · Updated October 15, 2026

Movora provides AI image and video creation, creation history, credits and purchases. This page explains the data used by this Android build.

Your creator identity

No registration or login is required. The app creates an anonymous creator identity and a random installation identifier to associate your history, credits and purchases. Access and refresh tokens are encrypted using Android Keystore. We do not request a Google sign-in token, email verification code or password. Google Play uses your Google account separately to process purchases.

Photos, videos and prompts

When you choose Generate, your selected media and prompt are uploaded to our backend and processed by the AI service providers used for that feature. The app asks for AI processing permission before your first creation. You can withdraw this permission in Settings > AI Processing; requests already sent may still finish. Only select media you have permission to use.

Service and purchase data

Our backend stores the creator identifier, submitted media, task progress, generated results, credit ledger and purchase entitlements needed to provide the service. Google Play processes payment information; Movora receives product identifiers and purchase tokens for server verification. We do not receive your full payment card information.

Device and local data

Service requests include app and Android versions, device model, language, time zone and network information such as IP address. The app caches catalog media and saves preferences, drafts and your AI processing choice. Android's photo picker gives access to the media you select. Saving a result uses Android's file picker; sharing uses the app you choose. This build does not include Google Sign-In, Firebase Analytics, Meta or Adjust SDKs.

Feedback and content reports

If you contact us or report generated content, we collect the message, report category, creator and creation reference, app version, and contact email if you choose to provide one. An email address is optional. These records help us investigate content, support requests and service issues.

Service providers

Supabase supplies authentication and backend infrastructure. AI providers process submitted inputs to deliver the requested creation. Google Play handles purchases and subscription management. Providers receive data needed for these services under their own applicable policies.

Audience and security

Movora is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Do not submit another person’s media without permission, or use the service to impersonate or harm others. Data is transmitted using HTTPS. Media submitted for processing may be handled by service providers in other countries. We retain creations and account information to provide your history and service until you delete them or request deletion; purchase, security and support records may be retained for applicable obligations or unresolved disputes.

Deletion and support

You can delete individual creations from their result screen, delete creation history from Settings, or delete your creator data from Settings > Account > Delete Account. Deleting the app or creator data does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Transaction or security records may be retained where required to administer purchases or comply with applicable obligations.

Uninstalling or clearing app data removes the local anonymous identity. Use Restore for purchases Google Play can restore; consumed credit packs and creation history may require support. Before reinstalling, keep your User ID from Settings > Account.

For privacy, access, correction or deletion requests, email berkehancanat@gmail.com with your User ID where available. Do not send session tokens, passwords or full payment details.
