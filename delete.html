Movora Android — Delete your creator data

No registration is needed to use Movora. The app creates an anonymous creator identity for your history, credits and purchases.

Delete in the app

Open History → Settings → your profile → Delete Account. This deletes the creator identity, uploaded media and creation history. To delete only a creation, open it from History and use Delete.

Request deletion without the app

Email berkehancanat@gmail.com with the subject “Movora Android data deletion”. Include your User ID if available (Settings → Account) and state whether you want all creator data or particular creations deleted. We may need information to verify ownership. Do not send passwords, session tokens or full payment card details.

Purchase, transaction or security records may be retained where needed to administer purchases, resolve disputes or meet applicable obligations. Deletion does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel recurring subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
